Essential Question
Why does dividing by 10, 100, or 1,000 make a number smaller?

Yesterday the digits slid left. Today they slide right. The same chart shows the opposite operation.

What the model showed

Dividing by 10 moves each digit one place right. Dividing by 100 moves each digit two places right. Dividing by 1,000 moves each digit three places right.

The value gets smaller because each digit lands in a smaller place.
