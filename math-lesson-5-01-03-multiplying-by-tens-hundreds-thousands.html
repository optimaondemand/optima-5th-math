Today's Mission
Build an area model that breaks apart a factor, shows partial products, and puts the pieces back together.
❓
Essential Question
How can an area model make a large multiplication problem easier to see?

A large product can feel like too much at once. The area model lets you split one number, multiply each part, and add the parts back together.

🎯
Today's Objectives
You will…
🔎Break apart a factor by place value.
🧮Build partial products with an area model.
💬Explain why the partial products add to the full product.
