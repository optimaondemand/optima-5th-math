Today's Mission
Use a grouping mat to divide by tens, hundreds, and thousands without losing the place value.
❓
Essential Question
How can grouping help us divide by 10, 100, or 1,000-sized numbers?

A division problem can be seen as a question: how many groups of this size can I make? Today the groups are tens, hundreds, or thousands.

🎯
Today's Objectives
You will…
🔎Build equal groups from a place-value amount.
🧮Connect hundreds and thousands to division facts.
💬Explain the quotient as the number of equal groups.

What the model showed

3,600 ÷ 300 asks, “How many groups of 300 fit into 3,600?”

Since 3,600 is 36 hundreds and 300 is 3 hundreds, the problem becomes: how many groups of 3 hundreds are in 36 hundreds?
