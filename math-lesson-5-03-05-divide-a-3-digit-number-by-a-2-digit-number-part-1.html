Today's Mission
Remove chunks of 12 and keep track of how many groups you used.

Essential Question
How can we divide 156 by taking away helpful groups of 12?

A teacher has 156 index cards. Each review packet needs 12 cards. How many review packets can be made?

The model will show what each number means before you calculate.
