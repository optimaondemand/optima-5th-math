Readiness Check
Two quick questions about fractions you already know.

1What is 2 × 13?

✅ Yes. Two copies of one third make two thirds.
🔄 Multiplying a fraction by a whole number repeats the fraction. 13 + 13 is 2 thirds. The pieces are still thirds, so the denominator stays 3.

2What is 12 × 13?

✅ Right. 1 × 1 = 1 and 2 × 3 = 6. Half of one third is one sixth.
🔄 Multiply the numerators: 1 × 1 = 1. Multiply the denominators: 2 × 3 = 6. Cut a third in half, and each piece is a sixth.

💡 What You Just Saw

The patio split into four parts: 2, 13, 1, and 16 square yards. The wholes make 3. Then 13 + 16 = 26 + 16 = 36, which is 12.

1 12 × 2 13 = 2 + 1 + 26 + 16 = 3 36 = 3 12

To multiply mixed numbers, split each one into a whole number and a fraction. Multiply each part by each part. That gives four partial products. Add all four.

📝
Worked Example: The Back Patio
A new patio, the same four steps.
Leo’s family adds a back patio 2 12 yards long and 1 13 yards wide. What is its area?
2121

1 × 1212

Side lengths are in yards. Not drawn to scale.
  1. Split each side. 2 12 = 2 + 12, and 1 13 = 1 + 13.
  2. Find the four parts. 1 × 2 = 2. 1 × 12 = 12. 13 × 2 = 23. 13 × 12 = 16.
  3. Make the fractions match. 12 = 36 and 23 = 46. So the fraction parts are 36 + 46 + 16 = 86.
  4. Add everything. 86 = 1 26. Then 2 + 1 26 = 3 26 = 3 13.
The back patio is 3 13 square yards.

1Which shows 2 13 split into a whole number and a fraction?

2Which is equal to 12 × 23?

✅ Right. 1 × 2 = 2 and 2 × 3 = 6, so the product is 26, which equals 13.
🔄 Multiply the numerators: 1 × 2 = 2. Multiply the denominators: 2 × 3 = 6. That gives 26. Divide both by 2 to find it in the choices.
