Today's Mission
Use a picture first. Then connect the picture to the multiplication sentence.
❓
Essential Question
How can a mixed-number product be built from smaller rectangles?

Fraction multiplication can look strange if we only stare at symbols. Today, the model does the talking first.

Look at the pieces, watch what changes, and then write the number sentence that matches.

🎯
Today's Objectives
You will…

• Build or study a visual model for the fraction product.

• Connect the picture to a multiplication sentence.

• Explain the answer with numbers and words.

You'll need:📓 Math Journal✏️ Pencil👀 Careful eyes
