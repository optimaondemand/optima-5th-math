Readiness Check
Two quick questions about feet, inches, and fractions.

1How many inches are in 1 foot?

✅ Yes. 1 foot = 12 inches. A school ruler is 1 foot long.
🔄 Picture a school ruler. It is 1 foot long. Count the inch marks from the start to the end and you get 12. So 1 foot = 12 inches. (16 is the number of ounces in a pound.)

2What is 13 of 12?

✅ Right. Split 12 into 3 equal parts and each part is 4.
🔄 13 means 1 of 3 equal parts. Share 12 into 3 equal groups: 4, 4, and 4. One group is 4. That is the same as 12 ÷ 3.

What You Just Saw

To change a fraction of a foot into inches, multiply the fraction by 12, because 1 foot = 12 inches. To change a fraction of a pound into ounces, multiply by 16, because 1 pound = 16 ounces.

23 × 12 = 243 = 8, so 23 ft = 8 in.
🧮
Worked Example
Return to the warm-up situation.

Back to the ribbon: 23 of a foot. How many inches is that?

  1. 1 foot = 12 inches. Draw a bar for 1 foot with a 12-inch strip under it.
  2. Cut the foot into 3 equal parts. Each third lines up with 12 ÷ 3 = 4 inches.
  3. Shade 2 thirds. They line up with 4 + 4 = 8 inches.
  4. 23 ft = 8 in.

Check: 23 × 12 = 243 = 8. Now build it in the tool and write the answer in a sentence.
