📐 Hemiunu’s Measuring Rules

Ones0.Tenths3Hundredths4Thousandths6
  • When 1 meter is divided into 10 equal parts, each part is 1 tenth (0.1 m = 1 dm).
  • When 1 tenth is divided into 10 equal parts, each part is 1 hundredth (0.01 m = 1 cm).
  • When 1 hundredth is divided into 10 equal parts, each part is 1 thousandth (0.001 m = 1 mm). We need a new place: the 3rd place after the decimal point.
  • 1 mm = 11,000 m, 1 mL = 11,000 L, 1 g = 11,000 kg.
  • Numbers less than 1 get a 0 in the ones place. Use zeros to hold empty places.

Each place is 10 times smaller than the place to its left. Divide a hundredth into 10 equal parts and you get thousandths, the 3rd place after the point. 1 mm = 0.001 m = 11,000 m.
