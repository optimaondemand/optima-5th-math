Today's Mission
Zoom into one hundredth, split it into 10 smaller pieces, and use that action to understand thousandths.
❓
Big Question
How can one tiny hundredth square become ten thousandths?

This lesson is written so you can work independently. Read each direction, use the model, then write what you noticed before you check the rule.

🎯
Today's Objectives
You will…
✅Identify thousandths as parts of a whole split into 1,000 equal pieces.
✅Use a zoomed grid to connect hundredths and thousandths.
✅Write and explain decimals to the thousandths place.

Centerpiece: Grid Zoomer
Click one hundredth, zoom in, then shade thousandths.
👀

What to do: First click the outlined square in the hundredths grid. That square will open into 10 tiny pieces. Then click tiny pieces to shade thousandths. Watch the decimal readout.

Step 1: Click the outlined hundredth square.

Step 2: Zoomed-in hundredth

This one hundredth has been split into 10 equal pieces. Each tiny piece is one thousandth.

0.000
Shade tiny pieces to build thousandths.
What the model means: When 10 thousandths are shaded, they fill the whole hundredth square. That is why 0.010 is the same amount as 0.01.
Explain what happened when you zoomed into one hundredth.
What You Just Saw

A thousandth is one of 1,000 equal parts of a whole. You can make thousandths by splitting each hundredth into 10 smaller equal pieces. Ten thousandths fill the same space as one hundredth.

Worked Example
Build 0.006 from thousandths.

To build 0.006, shade 6 tiny thousandth pieces. Since each piece is one thousandth, 6 pieces are 6 thousandths.

Read it aloud: six thousandths.
