📐 Hemiunu’s Place-Value Rules

Ones1.Tenths4Hundredths8Thousandths6
  • The value of a digit depends on its place. In 1.486, the 8 is worth 8 hundredths, or 0.08.
  • 10 thousandths = 1 hundredth. 10 hundredths = 1 tenth. 10 tenths = 1 one.
  • Each place is worth 10 times as much as the place to its right. Each place is 110 of the place to its left.
  • Expanded form adds the values: 1.486 = 1 + 0.4 + 0.08 + 0.006 = 1 + 410 + 8100 + 61,000.
  • A 0 holds an empty place. It adds nothing, so it gets no part in expanded form.
