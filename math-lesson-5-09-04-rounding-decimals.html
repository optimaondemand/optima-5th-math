Essential Question

When is a measurement close enough?

Find the two benchmarks. The number rounds to the one it is closer to.

Today's Objectives

You will…

  • Round decimals to the nearest whole number, tenth and hundredth.
  • Use a number line and the digit to the right to decide.
  • Explain why one number can round to different values.

Three stops today. First, learn to round a measurement. Next, fix my scribes’ mix-ups. Then write the work orders on your own.

LEARN IT Round to the nearest place

👥 Math Crew: four ways to round 4.273 to the nearest hundredth

4.273 is between 4.27 and 4.28. Halfway is 4.275.
The digit to the right is 3. 3 is less than 5, so 4.27.
On the number line, 4.273 is before halfway. It’s closer to 4.27.
4.273 ≈ 4.27. The ≈ sign means “is about.”

📐 Hemiunu’s Rounding Rules

Ones1.Tenths6Hundredths3Thousandths8
  • Find the rounding place. Name the two benchmarks: 1.638 is between 1.6 and 1.7.
  • Look at the digit one place to the right.
  • 0 to 4: keep the rounding digit. 5 to 9: round up. Halfway rounds up.
  • Drop the digits after the rounding place. Keep a 0 if it sits in the rounding place: 3.96 ≈ 4.0.
  • Nearest hundredth of a meter = nearest centimeter. Nearest tenth = nearest decimeter.

My scribes made three mix-ups that kids often make too. Study the solved example first, then fix each one.

🔍 Study It: round 7.096 to the nearest hundredth

Already solved. Read both ways, then fix the mix-ups.

Way 1: number line

7.096 is between 7.09 and 7.10. Halfway is 7.095.

7.096 is past halfway, so it is closer to 7.10.

Way 2: the digit to the right

The hundredths digit is 9. The digit to its right is 6, so round up.

9 hundredths + 1 hundredth = 10 hundredths = 1 tenth. So 7.096 ≈ 7.10. The 0 shows it was rounded to hundredths.

🔍 Study It: which lengths round to 2.4?

To the nearest tenth, a number rounds to 2.4 if it is closer to 2.4 than to 2.3 or 2.5. That means from the halfway mark 2.35 up to just before 2.45.

2.35 ≈ 2.4 ✓   2.449 ≈ 2.4 ✓   2.45 ≈ 2.5 ✗   2.349 ≈ 2.3 ✗
