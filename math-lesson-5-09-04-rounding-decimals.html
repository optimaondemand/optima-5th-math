Readiness Check: Benchmarks
Rounding is easier when you know the two choices.

If you round 3.472 to the nearest hundredth, the two benchmark choices are 3.47 and 3.48. The answer is the one 3.472 is closer to.

What You Just Saw

To round a decimal, first choose the rounding place. Then find the two benchmark values the number is between. The rounded number is the benchmark that is closer.

Worked Example
Round 4.683 to the nearest hundredth.

The hundredths benchmarks are 4.68 and 4.69. The number 4.683 is closer to 4.68 than to 4.69, so it rounds to 4.68.
