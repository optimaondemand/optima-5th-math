Dividing Decimals by 10, 100, and 1,000

Big Question: How can a decimal become 10, 100, or 1,000 times smaller?

Key Concept

Dividing by 10, 100, or 1,000 makes a decimal value smaller. Each digit moves to a smaller place. The decimal point stays fixed.

Worked Example
24.8 ÷ 100

Dividing by 100 means two right slides. 2 tens become 2 tenths. 4 ones become 4 hundredths. 8 tenths become 8 thousandths.

So 24.8 ÷ 100 = 0.248.
