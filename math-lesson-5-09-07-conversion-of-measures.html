Centerpiece: Metric Staircase
Move a measurement up or down and watch the number change.

What to do: Place a measurement on a unit step. Then move it to a new step. Count each stair step as ×10 or ÷10.

👀

The length does not change. Only the unit changes. Smaller units mean you need more of them, so the number gets larger.

Key Concept

When you convert a measurement, the real-world length stays the same. The number changes because the size of the unit changes. Moving to smaller units uses multiplication. Moving to larger units uses division.

Worked Example
650 centimeters to meters

Centimeters are smaller than meters. To go from centimeters up to meters, move two steps up. Two steps up means divide by 100.

So 650 cm = 6.5 m.
