Centerpiece: Strategy Picker
Choose a smart path, then build the steps.

What to do: Solve 4.5 × 20 by choosing a strategy. Mental math does not mean magic. It means choosing a path that makes the numbers easier.

Double then ×10

First double 4.5. Then multiply by 10.

Place-value slide

Think of 20 as 2 × 10.

Break apart 20

Think of 20 as 10 + 10.

Choose a strategy card to open a workspace.
Which strategy did you choose, and why?
Key Concept

Efficient mental math uses number relationships you already know. A strategy is correct when every step keeps the value equal and leads to the same answer.

Worked Example
0.75 × 200

One smart path is to think of 200 as 2 × 100. First double 0.75 to get 1.5. Then multiply 1.5 by 100 to get 150.

So 0.75 × 200 = 150.
