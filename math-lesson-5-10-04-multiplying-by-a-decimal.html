🔎

The tricky corner: The small corner is 0.4 by 0.3. That means 4 tenths by 3 tenths. Tenths times tenths make hundredths, so the corner is 12 hundredths, or 0.12.

✏️ Explain why the tiny corner is 0.12 instead of 12 or 1.2.
📐 What You Just Saw

A decimal area model works like a whole-number area model. First build the side lengths. Then split each side into whole-number and decimal parts. Last, find the area of each smaller rectangle and add the pieces together.

The decimal places matter because tenths times tenths make hundredths. That is why 0.4 × 0.3 is 0.12, not 1.2 or 12.
