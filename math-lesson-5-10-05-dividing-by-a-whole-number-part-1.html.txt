✏️ Why does 36 tenths shared into 3 groups make 1.2 in each group?
📐 What You Just Saw
Dividing by a whole number can mean sharing a decimal amount equally.
Since 3.6 is 36 tenths, sharing 36 tenths into 3 equal groups gives 12 tenths in each group. Twelve tenths is 1.2.
