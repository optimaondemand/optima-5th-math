✏️ Why does the decimal point in the quotient line up with the decimal point in 5.28?
📐 What You Just Saw
The algorithm records the same sharing you did in the model.
Wholes are shared first, then tenths, then hundredths.
The decimal point lines up because the quotient has ones, tenths, and hundredths in the same order as the dividend.
