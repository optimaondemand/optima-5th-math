Centerpiece: Real Protractor Alignment
Line up the actual tool before reading the scale.
This model now shows a real semicircle protractor with tick marks and degree numbers. The angle is 62°. Use the three steps to see why the placement matters.
1. Center dot on vertex
2. Zero line on one ray
3. Other ray crosses 62°
Measured angle: 62°
✏️ Why do the center dot and zero line have to be aligned before the degree number makes sense?
📐 What You Just Saw

A protractor has to match the angle before the number means anything. Center on vertex. Baseline on one ray. Then read where the other ray crosses the scale.
