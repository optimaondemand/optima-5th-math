Today's Mission
Tear off the three corners of a triangle and arrange them to make 180°.
❓
Essential Question
Why do the angles in a triangle add to 180°?

Today the triangle corners move. When the three angles are rearranged side by side, they form a straight angle.

Visual Proof: Triangle Angles Make a Straight Line
A static model is clearer than a fake tear-off tool.
Instead of tearing pieces off on the screen, this model shows the same idea as a clean proof: copy the three corner angles from the triangle, then place the copied angles beside each other on a straight line.

What the picture proves

The three angles inside any triangle add to 180°. Their sizes can change, but together they always fill one straight line.

✏️ Explain why the straight line at the bottom shows that the triangle angles total 180°.
📐 What You Just Saw

The three angles in a triangle make one straight angle. A straight angle is 180°, so the angles in any triangle add to 180°.
