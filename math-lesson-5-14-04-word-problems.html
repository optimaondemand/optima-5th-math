Today's Mission
Use double number lines and rate tables to find unit rates, compare rates, and solve stories.
❓
Big Question
How can two aligned number lines show how quantities change together?

Today's lesson uses Rate Model Workspace. Build the model first, then use words and numbers to explain what happened.

Math Crew — What Do They Think?
Four ways to understand today's idea.
🧭 Atlas

I notice rates grow in two directions at once. The matching values need to stay lined up.

🔧 Felix

I check a rate by finding one unit. Once I know the unit rate, I can test the rest of the table.

🎨 Hazel

📝 Cora
