Type each gray word in the Models search box. Put out the number shown. Skip signs that say “or Equal.”

🥽 In VR
Step 2 of 7 · Go in
🔗 First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com. The steps are in Having trouble in VR? below.

Open the On-Demand app. Click Explore. Type Giza in Filter environments. Click Pyramids of Giza, then Begin Solo.

Step 3 of 7 · Build the chisels

Pick an open spot with the pyramids behind you. Open your menu (top button, right controller) and click Models.

Chisel A, 0.312 m: build it on the left with Flats, Rods and Units in a row.

Chisel B, 0.32 m: build it on the right the same way.

✋ Grab a model with the palm trigger. Use both palm triggers to resize it.
Step 4 of 7 · Write the answer with models

Under the chisels, write Chisel A’s length, the sign, then Chisel B’s length with Number models. Each decimal point is a Centimeter Cube, set low.

🗣 Say it out loud: which place decides? (Optional in a video.)
Step 5 of 7 · Extra, if you have time

VR only · not graded Build Chisel C, 0.302 m. Line up all three chisels from shortest to longest.

Step 6 of 7 · Snap or record, then submit

Stand back so both chisels and your answer fit. Open your menu, click Library, then Camera. Take a photo, or switch to Video and record up to 30 seconds.

Click the Canvas tab and pick 5.09.03 VR · Chisel Check. Click Choose from My Files, pick your photo or video, click Use this file, then Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag.

Turn in one photo or one video. That is the only thing you turn in.

Drafting Table · no headset

Check the chisels

Hemiunu · Master BuilderNo headset today? Then come to my drafting table. Build both chisels here, then write me a full report on what you found.
👀 What am I looking at? A place value mat has a column for each place. A Flat is 1 tenth, a Rod is 1 hundredth and a Unit is 1 thousandth, just like in VR.
What to do: Pick a piece, then tap its column (or drag it there). Press Check when the chisel is built.
✍️ Your math paragraph
A math paragraph explains how you solved it. Write 5 sentences or more (60 words or more):
  1. What did you build, and what is the answer?
  2. How did you solve it? Use the numbers.
  3. How do you know it’s right?
  4. What mix-up could someone make, and why is it wrong?
  5. Use 2 of our math words.
